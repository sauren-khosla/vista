# Vista 0.1.0 — First public preview

A quiet, native eye-break companion for macOS. A tiny floating dot expands into a gentle reminder to take a 20-second distance break.

- A 20-minute default rhythm with configurable intervals, snooze, and pause.
- A movable widget that remembers its position across launches.
- Automatic light/dark appearance, Reduced Motion support, and a soft completion chime.
- Dock and menu-bar controls, plus optional launch at login.
- No accounts, telemetry, camera, or network access.

## Download & install

Download **Vista-0.1.0-universal.dmg**, open it, and drag **Vista** to **Applications**. Eject the disk image and open Vista from Applications.

Supports **macOS 14 or newer**, on **Apple silicon and Intel**.

**This preview is not notarized by Apple.** It is ad-hoc signed. If macOS blocks the first launch and you choose to trust it, attempt to open Vista, then use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. [Apple's instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mh40616/mac).

`SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the disk image checksum. Intel is cross-compiled and included in the universal binary; it has not been manually exercised on a physical Intel Mac.
